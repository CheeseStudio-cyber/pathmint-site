Pathmint Support

Simple help for Pathmint on iPhone.

Pathmint helps you break goals into paths, complete steps in order, earn points, unlock mystery rewards, and redeem rewards from your personal Reward Shop.

Common questions

How many paths can I create on the Free plan?

The Free plan allows up to 3 paths in total. Active, completed, and archived paths all count toward this limit.

How many nodes can a Free path contain?

Free users can add up to 5 nodes to each path. Pathmint Pro removes this limit.

How many Reward Shop rewards can I create?

The Free plan allows up to 3 Reward Shop rewards in total, including inactive rewards. Pathmint Pro removes this limit.

How do I restore Pathmint Pro?

Open Pathmint, go to Settings, choose Upgrade to Pro, then tap Restore Purchases. Restore Purchases can restore an eligible Pathmint Pro purchase associated with your Apple Account.

What happens after a refund?

If Apple refunds or revokes a Pathmint Pro purchase, the Pro entitlement is no longer valid and cannot be restored unless a valid entitlement exists again.

Where is my data stored?

Pathmint currently stores app data locally on your device. No Pathmint account is required.
