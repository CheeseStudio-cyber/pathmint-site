Pathmint Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 13, 2026

Pathmint is designed to work without requiring an account. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the Pathmint iOS app.

Information we collect

Pathmint does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data with the developer or third-party advertising or analytics services.

Data stored on your device

Your goals, paths, tasks, rewards, point history, settings, and related app content are stored locally on your device using Apple platform storage technologies. Pathmint does not operate a backend account system or server that receives this data.

In-App Purchases

Pathmint Pro purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Pathmint does not receive or store your payment card details. The app checks Apple-provided purchase entitlement information to determine whether Pathmint Pro is unlocked.

Analytics and advertising

Pathmint does not currently use third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs.

Links to other websites

You may choose to save external web links inside tasks. Opening those links may take you to third-party websites or services that have their own privacy practices. Pathmint is not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties.

Data deletion

You can delete paths and rewards from within Pathmint. Because app data is stored locally, removing the app from your device may also remove locally stored app data, subject to Apple device backup and restore behavior.

Children's privacy

Pathmint does not knowingly collect personal information from children because the app does not collect personal data from users.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Pathmint adds features such as cloud sync, account creation, analytics, or other services that change how data is handled, this privacy policy will be updated accordingly.
